## Project decisions

- Keep the marketing homepage as one smooth-scrolling route because the approved experience connects all primary sections directly from the hero and navigation.
- Keep service content in a shared catalog and render an overview plus dynamic detail pages under the Services layout so navigation, forms and deliverables stay consistent.
- Use shared site navigation on homepage and service pages so the Services dropdown and homepage section links work throughout the site.
